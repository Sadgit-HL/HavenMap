## Architecture and data

- 2026-11-26: Class tokens (owner): counted tokens have maximums (Soothsinger notes 2, Shattersong, Hail, Deepwraith and Blinkblade 5, Quartermaster supply 50 in steps of 10); toggle tokens go forward on a left click and back on a right click; Geminate has a Melee/Ranged toggle (the two condition pictures); the pressure toggle belongs to Metal Mosaic, H.I.V.E. has none. Prescriptions of the Sawbones are given to another character (`rx` on the owner, the token is a marker of the owner).
- 2026-11-26: Typed card actions (owner, option B): the free-text lines of the card code are parsed into typed actions by a generator into `scripts/games/card_actions.js`; the card code JSON in `card-code-current/` is not edited, because it is the owner's checked source. Lines the parser does not understand completely stay free text. First kinds: experience, recover items and cards, create terrain, obstacle, trap and token. Next: a trigger type (condition, timing, count) for the "when" and "next N" lines, then custom handlers per card. Reference for the design: the ability and event structure of HoogeboomBas/TheCrimsonScales (MIT).
- 2026-11-25: The card data is for later automation (owner): the game meaning of every action on every card has to be right; the format does not matter. The card pictures are the only truth. Two readings of a card are compared by meaning (`tools/card-extraction/compare.py`), not by wording or layout. A confirmed class can still hold errors: the owner's Berserker code had push 2 on Into the Fight, the picture shows pull 2 (fixed).
- 2026-11-25: Initiative: where the app's value (read from the card pictures) and the Secretariat's differ, the app's wins (owner; checked on the pictures: Demolitionist Level 61, Extra Fuel 17, Red Guard Soaring Shield 11). `convert-secretariat-cards.py` takes the app's value. It leaves a value alone when the app's value belongs to another card of the deck (the Tinkerer's Potent Potables and Disorienting Flash, whose names the Secretariat has wrong; `NAME_FIX`).
- 2026-11-25: Icon names in card code (owner): `plague` is the Plagueherald condition (yellow hand in a pentagon); Metal Mosaic pressure: `gain_pressure` (red up arrow), `lose_pressure` (blue down arrow), `low_pressure` (blue gauge), `regular_pressure` (green), `high_pressure` (yellow), `over_pressure` (red gauge, also drawn crossed); `self_dmg` is the red box with a gear star; `regenerate` is the pink or purple diamond (Infuser, Metal Mosaic); `infusion` is the Infuser's rosette. These are names only: the rules the engine applies to them are still to be written. The Obsidian Spear pill is pull 1, the Remote Impact pill push 1.
- 2026-11-25: Supply (owner): the Sawbones' six medical packs and the Quartermaster's items QM1 to QM9 have a supply of their own. A card or item is lent to a character and goes back to the supply as soon as it is discarded (a pack: discard or lost pile; an item: spent or consumed). Lent packs do not count against the hand limit and lent items take no slot. The supply is seen and edited only on the Sawbones and Quartermaster Mercenaries tabs, by any player. Which medical pack is given is random (the app's draw or a GeekRoller code, as the roll mode says). The packs are not part of the normal Sawbones deck. Scenario end is not modelled; if it is, lent cards return then.
- 2026-11-25: Turn post template (owner): level 3 detail, short: one line per action with icons and values, stickers laid on the card (markers, not the enhancement log) as `+N` on the value, no draw line and no rolls (the player adds rolls inline), a dashed pill in parentheses right after its action, no "from X" for moves (`to X`), no hex areas or empty dots; card text copied as printed; a last line per action with the experience and persistent, round, lost, removed. Generated from the card code, so it is as correct as the card code of the class (Hail is confirmed). Details in `playmat.md`.
- 2026-11-25: Infusion cards (owner): the keyword at the top left of a half means the elements in its header must be consumed to play the card into the active area; the text to the right of it is a persistent effect (like an augment); the other abilities of the half trigger whenever an infusion is placed in the active area. Written as `namedAbility: Infusion`, a half-level `consume: [air, wild]` (`wild` is any element), the persistent text above the divider (JSON `augment`) and the triggered abilities below it. A banner of consume icons with a colon is a plain `mandatory` consume. A split icon of two elements (earth and dark) means either.

- 2026-11-21: Draws with advantage and disadvantage (owner; the Frosthaven rulebook text and FAQ as the owner plays them). Cards are drawn until one that is not rolling (N), then one more (C) whose rolling icon is ignored. Advantage: a character picks N or C, a monster (the monster and the ally deck) takes the better one automatically, and all rolling cards drawn first are added to the card that counts. Disadvantage: the worse of N and C is taken automatically, the rolling cards are ignored. Comparing (`amdCompare.js`): numbers; x2 counts as +attack value (a field in the dialog), Miss is worse than every card; a non-numeric effect is positive but unknown, so an effect card is better than a plain card with an equal or lower number, two cards with the same effect are compared by number, different effects are ambiguous; ambiguous and equal give N, the card drawn first. Conditional bonuses and items are not known to the app: the dialog can switch an automatic choice by hand. Adv and Dis buttons beside Draw (hero deck, opened Monsters view; also in the Monsters strip on desktop); History says "(random, advantage)" and a line names the card that counts. Owner.
