- Overview: party, prosperity (checks, Great Oak sanctuary, imbuement), reputation, scenario counts, stickers, unlocked classes, players, retired characters. Popups from pencils.

Unlocked scenarios with a treasure nobody took show a gold chest in the upper left corner of their flowchart box (with the number left when more than one). Tested in Playwright.
- Scenarios: table and flowchart (`scripts/campaign/flow.js`, `flowPage`), state, result, unlock links both ways. Flowchart (2026-11-18): pan with right-drag, left-drag on the background or Space plus drag; Ctrl+wheel steps the sizes S to XXL around the pointer; the scroll position survives re-renders; a search field (number or name, with suggestions) centers and marks a scenario, locked boxes are not found. Tested in Playwright (Edge, 1440x900). Added 2026-11-18: lane labels stay at the left edge while panning; the chart opens on the saved position (`view.flowPos`), else on the scenario unlocked by the last completed one; an overview map in the lower right corner (click or drag moves the view); lane order chosen for the shortest arrows (`LANES` in `flow.js`). Storylines checked against the Secretariat and the owner's GH2 Flow sheet; 13 Icecrag Ascent moved to Voice and Drake, 1, 2 and 3 to Guards, the Other lane is empty. Sticker look (2026-11-18, mockup `campaign/mockups/scenario-look.html`, variants 1C and 3A): boxes are sticker cards (local art from `images/stickers/gh2`, number and name band, state dot), storyline colours (`LANE_COLOR` in `flow.js`) on lanes, lane labels, boxes, tags and link chips; the scenario modal has a banner with the sticker, storyline and state tags, and linked scenarios as sticker chips that open their page. Chart sizes rescaled (S 0.45 to XXL 1.4). Phone list rows have the sticker thumbnail ("?" while locked), a storyline stripe and the storyline name with its colour; the modal shrinks its banner on short screens. Tested in Playwright (Edge, 1440x900; touch emulation 390x844 and 844x390: list, modal, flowchart). Added 2026-11-20: storylines now come from the owner's flow image (`scenario_flow.jpeg`); a scenario can have two (`area` "Guards/Hail", first colour first; `lanesOf` in `flow.js`) and is drawn in the lane holding most of its linked scenarios (tie: first colour), with a split two-colour stripe, two tags and a filter that matches either. Forced links (`forced`, 13 red arrows of the image) get a chain icon on the middle of the arrow (`FLOW_LINK`); boss scenarios (`boss`, 15 skulls of the image) get a skull icon (`SKULL`) in the Table, on unlocked flowchart boxes and as a Boss tag on the scenario page.
