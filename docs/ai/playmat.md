# Mercenaries tab (playmat) and phone view

The Mercenaries tab of the campaign page is the most used screen during a scenario. It is built as a playmat: a card table with the character's hand, play area, active area, piles, item shelf and a dock of modal buttons. Phone widths get a separate layout with the same data and functions.

Code: `campaign/playmat.js` (markup, modals, dock, settings), `campaign/playmat.css` (hand-maintained), `campaign/app.js` (wiring, state in `pm`), `campaign/ctxmenu.js`, `campaign/morph.js`, `campaign/phone.js` and `phone.css`. Data: `campaign-record.md`. Visual rules: `ui-guidelines.md`. Mockups for the design history: `campaign/mockups/` (`merc-6-playmat-v2.html` is the desktop reference, `merc-7-phone-v2.html` the phone reference).

## Requirements (from the owner's original brief, since deleted from the workspace)

1. Cards: drag to discard and Lost piles; hand area; active area; enhancements shown on cards.
2. Attack modifier deck of the character (modal, now in the hand area).
3. Item area.
4. Perks and masteries (modal).
5. Personal quest and notes (journal modal).
6. Battle goal (journal modal).
7. Header: class, name, player, level, XP with thresholds, gold, inherited perks, traits.
8. Character mat, front and back (modal).
9. Class portrait.
10. Easy copy of BGG codes.
11. Notes.
12. Custom class counters (resolution, time tokens). Partly done as class special tokens.
13. Enhancement table (editable).

## Layout (desktop, `.pm`)

- Top bar: portrait, name, class, traits, level badge, XP row with the bar, gold and inherited perks spin boxes, class tokens, BGG code buttons (AMD, Turn, Cards), settings. One line at 1400 px and wider. Below 1400 px the BGG buttons show icons only.
- Left: item shelf.
- Board: top row has the Active area (left) and two play slots (right, slot 1 sets initiative). Hand below, with header (`n of hand size`, Spread, Fan or Grid, Sort by initiative, Hand or Modifiers switch).
- Right: Discard and Lost piles (top card shown, click opens the pile modal).
- Far right: dock, six modal buttons: Perks and masteries, Journal, Character mat, BGG codes, Enhancements, History. (Attack modifiers are in the hand bar.)
- Card size: `--cu` set by `sizePlaymat()` in `app.js`, 14% of stage height or 12% of width, 60 to 160 px.
- Card preview: every playmat card (Spread hand, Active area, face-up play cards, items, Modifiers view, pile, hand editor, item and perks modals) uses the shared card preview of all tabs (see "Card preview" below). Only the Fan zooms in place: each fan card has an inner face `.acf` that zooms, while the `.ac` stays in place as the hover target, so a card zoomed away from the pointer does not flicker. The fan never clips its cards (overflow visible). With "Show on: Ctrl+hover" the fan zooms in place only while Ctrl is held (`data-fanzoom="ctrl"` and the `.ctrlheld` class on `<html>`, set in `app.js`); Ctrl also skips the hover delay. A pinned fan card (Z, context menu Zoom) uses the preview.

## Zone rules (owner decisions)

- Every deck card is in exactly one zone: hand, active area, play slot, discard, lost.
- A card in the active area is not in the hand.
- Play slots take hand cards only. The two slots can swap. A card entering an occupied slot sends the old card back to the hand.
- Slot 1 sets initiative. Cards in the play area lie face down until flipped (click flips).
- The hand holds up to 14 cards, sorted by initiative (automatic) or in manual order (`hord`). Dragging a card in the hand sets manual order. Ini returns to automatic.
- Grid layout (2026-11-19): 6 fixed columns, at least 2 rows, one more row only when every cell is taken. Cards are dragged onto any cell (a taken cell swaps; a card from another zone takes the cell only if free). Positions are saved per character as `hgrid` (`{ cardId: cell }`), so they follow the record to other devices. Cards without a cell fill the free cells in hand order. Ini and Lvl sort clear `hgrid` and pack the cards again. Code: `handGrid` in `record.js`, `placeInHand` in `edit.js`, `.hgi` and `.hcell` in `playmat.css`. Hand order and grid changes are not written to the History (2026-11-19, owner). Desktop only; the phone view has its own layout.
- Drag between zones works without the Edit toggle: moving cards is play, not setup.
- Drag (2026-11-18): pointer events for mouse, touch and pen, no native HTML drag. The mouse picks a card up after 5 px, touch and pen after a 220 ms hold. The ghost (`ghostStart` in `campaign/app.js`, `.dragghost` in `style.css`) is an opaque copy, tilted 3 degrees with a shadow and a gold rim, at a fixed size: 1.5 card units (`--cu`) wide for ability cards, 1.1 for modifier cards (painted onto a canvas). The source stays as a dimmed, dashed placeholder. A drop fades the ghost; Esc or a drop outside a zone flies it back. Native drag remains only for the Events tab rows.
- End of round: drag each play card to discard. No dedicated control (owner decision).
- Short rest (2026-11-20, `scripts/campaign/rest.js`, `shortRest` and `shortRestRedo` in `edit.js`): one random card of the discard pile is lost and the rest of the pile returns to the hand; a dialog shows the lost card and offers Redo (take 1 damage): the first card goes back to the hand and is not picked again, another card of the pile is lost. One redo per rest, only while no other card of the pile was moved. Needs 2 cards in the discard pile. Started from the Discard pile dialog (Short rest button), the pile's right-click menu, the phone pile sheet, or R with the pointer over the discard pile. Both also work by a BGG roll (`restRollCode` in `rest.js`): the Short rest by BGG roll button and Redo by BGG roll open a dialog with the cards and a Copy code button (`1custom<n>{names}`, at most 241 characters, names shortened if needed); a click on the card the roll gave loses it (`shortRest` and `shortRestRedo` take it as `pick`). The state is `chars[].rest` (one History line per rest and per redo, naming a BGG roll). Long rest and lose-a-card: by hand, by dragging cards.
- Inherited perks are a number only.

## Modifier deck (Modifiers view in the hand bar)

- Deck stacks, Top and Bottom strips, the Draw button and the roll code button, discard pile (top card, modal), active area. A random draw opens a dialog with the cards drawn in order (rolling ones marked) and Draw again. The Std / Adv / Dis button next to the Draw button sets how the next draws go (a click cycles it; it stays until changed). The Draw button alone starts a draw: the first card shows at once, every further card (after a rolling card, or the second card of advantage or disadvantage) is drawn by a click on the face-down card in the dialog. With advantage or disadvantage the dialog shows the rolling cards, the last two cards and the one that counts (the player clicks it with advantage of a hero, else it is automatic, with a button to switch it); see `amdCompare.js` and decisions.md. An empty deck is shuffled at once during these draws. The shuffle button has a gold dot while a reshuffle is due (x2 or Miss in the discard, or nothing left to draw; `shuffleDue`). Space toggles Hand and Modifiers when nothing is focused.
- Draws, returns, shuffle, bless and curse, Top, Bottom, Active and count edits are saved in the record (`chars[].amd`, since 2026-11-18): every action is an edit (`ed.charAm` in `scripts/campaign/edit.js`), so it is shared, undone with Ctrl+Z and written to History (lines from `describeHeroDeck` in `changelog.js`). A class change clears it.
- Deck follows the taken perks (`c.pk`) via `scripts/campaign/amdDeck.js`. Roll codes: `1custom<n>{faces}` for the pool; `Top: X` and `Bottom: X` when a known card is placed.
- Every move writes a History line.
- Odds strip (2026-11-18): a framed box at the left end of the Top/Bottom row (`.amstats`, built in `amViewHtml`) with the chances of the next random draw from the pool: Miss, <=-2, <=-1, 0, >=+1, >=+2, Crit. Cumulative: <=-2 and <=-1 include misses, >=+1 and >=+2 include crits. Computed by `drawOdds(A)` in `amdDeck.js` from the `drawState` result. Bless counts as x2 (crit), Curse as Miss. Card effects are ignored, only the modifier value counts. Rolling cards are chained exactly (value added, draw again, without replacement; if only rolling cards remain, their sum is the result). Unique class cards take the value before the colon of their face text (`uniqueFaceText.js`, "+2: ...", "2x: ..."), else +0; a `>` in the face marks rolling. Variable cards ("+1/lost card in area") count as +0. Cards placed on Top or Bottom are not in the pool. The Std / Adv / Dis toggle is shown in both roll modes (it carries no `onlyApp()`): with GeekRoller it changes only these odds, the draws themselves use the roll code. With the deck's Adv or Dis mode on (2026-11-24, `drawOdds(A, mode)`) the strip shows the odds of the card that counts: the draw goes on to the first card that is not rolling (N, rolling cards before it R), one more card C comes from what is left (its rolling icon ignored); advantage counts the better of N and C plus R, disadvantage the worse and no R. By number only: x2 is above every number and Miss below every number (owner), card effects are ignored. A hero chooses with advantage, so the strip shows the best of two for a hero. With no card left after N, N counts alone (the game would reshuffle the discard first, the strip does not). Checked in Node (tiny decks, the monster deck, rolling cards) and in headless Chromium on the Monsters tab (Std, Adv, Dis). No average is shown (x2 and Miss depend on the attack value). The strip hides when the pool is empty. Same view on the Monsters tab. Placement mockups: `campaign/mockups/amd-stats.html` (variant 5 chosen). Odds math checked in Node; not checked in a browser.
- Layout (2026-11-17, `style.css`, block "Modifiers view, responsive layout"): sizes follow the view's box in container units. Deck stacks are sized so the deck fits two rows (`--perrow` set in `amViewHtml`); Top, Bottom and Active share one strip height (`--striph`), and full strips overlap their cards. Side column: one control row (draw or roll code, shuffle, edit), the discard fills the rest. Below 820 px window height the view takes the whole board: the Active/Play row and the ability card piles (Discard, Lost) are hidden while it is open. Checked in Playwright at 1024x640 to 1920x960 (window inner sizes).

## Modals and buttons

| Modal | What it does |
|---|---|
| Perks and masteries | checkmark counter on top (18 boxes, 6 cells of 3; a full cell is one earned perk, `pk.chk`); perk rows from the class perk list, taken perks change the deck at once; multi-box perks count when all boxes are ticked; masteries from `scripts/games/masteries.js` (Jaws of the Lion has none) |
| Journal | three tabs: Battle goals (3 drawn, keep 1; roll code `3custom<n>{ids}`; pictures from worldhaven, laid out like the quest tab), Personal quest (2 drawn, keep 1; quests shared by the party: `bgs`, `pq` in the record), Notes (markdown, toolbar, preview) |
| Character mat | front and back, click to flip |
| BGG codes | icon codes in blocks (General, Conditions, Elements, Loot, Terrain, Class specific), class banner codes (`[inlineimg]url[/inlineimg]`), user's own codes (localStorage `havenmap.campaign.codes`) |
| Enhancements | the class's enhancement log (`r.enh`), add with card and top, bottom or gold, price per row, remove |
| History | this character's entries from `record.log`, kind filter |
| Edit character | name, player, event traits, class change (restarts the character), portrait |
| Hand editor (deck modal) | class cards by level with states Locked, Unlocked, Hand; Confirm hand applies it. Level stepper moves XP to the level threshold |
| Items | state buttons (available, spent, consumed). Inventory modal (formerly Equip items, 2026-11-19) with an Equip and Sell switch: Equip, a click on the picture equips or unequips at once (slot limits: head 1, body 1, legs 1, hands 2, small items half the level rounded up; equipped items show the frame, others the plain picture, no Clear or Confirm buttons). Sell: a Sell button per item asks for confirmation, then the item returns to the shop and the character gets half its cost, rounded down; the toolbar shows the value of all items (`Value of all items N gold`). Items without a cost (not coin items) cannot be sold |
| Pile modal | recover top or all cards; a click on a card recovers it |
| Supply | only on the Sawbones and Quartermaster tabs (dock, phone menu): the six medical packs or the items QM1 to QM9 as tiles, the lent ones greyed with the holder's name (click to take back). "Give to" picks the receiver; a click on a Quartermaster item lends it; the Sawbones draws a medical pack with Draw medical pack (This app) or Draw by BGG roll (GeekRoller: copy the code, click the pack the roll gave). A lent pack in a play slot shows the Sawbones card back when face down. Lent packs are not counted in the hand count and limit. Data and rules: `scripts/campaign/supply.js`, `record.sup`, `decisions.md` 2026-11-25 |
| Markers | tokens and stickers on cards and items |

Turn post (2026-11-25, owner decision on the detail): for a card with a template (`scripts/games/turn_templates.js`: 1258 cards of all 43 classes (not the Sawbones' six medical packs), see data-pipeline.md; the card code of most classes is not checked yet) the Turn post has the lines of the card half instead of the `X` placeholder: slot 1 card, top half; slot 2 card, bottom half. One line per action with BGG icons and the printed value; range, jump and conditions stay on the line; the target of an attack, heal, push or pull is `on X`, the destination of a move or teleport `to X`; a dashed pill is written in parentheses right after the action it belongs to (a pill that stands alone has its own line); card text is copied as printed. The stickers laid on the card (the Markers dialog, `c.mk['c:<card id>']`, `s: 1`, x and y in percent of the picture; not the enhancement log `record.enh`) count: those above the initiative band (y below 54) go with the top half, the others with the bottom half. A sticker goes to the action line with enhancement dots that its height points to (the dotted lines spread evenly over the half; the template does not know where the dots are on the picture); a +1 adds `+1` to the first value of that line, not the range (`6+1`, two of them `6+2`), any other sticker is written after the action as its icon (`hex_attack` as `+hex`). A last line per action gives the half's experience (`[xp] 2`) and what happens to the card: persistent, round, lost, removed (owner, 2026-11-25); icons from the BGG codes list (`lost` 9944319, `persistent` 9944320, `removed` 9944321, `round` 9944322; 20 px pictures made from the cards, `tools/bgg-icons-upload/`, in the General block of `merc-6-playmat-v2.html`; a flag without an icon is a word). No hex areas, empty enhancement dots or class token ribbons. Cards without a template keep the placeholder. `turnPost` in `playmat.js`, parts in `scripts/turnTemplate.js` (`halfLines`), Node checks in `tools/tests/turnTemplate.test.mjs`. Not checked in a browser; the stickers are placed by the dotted-line rule, not by their position on the dots.

BGG posts: Turn (banner, top action in slot 1, bottom action in slot 2, `XXX` for empty) and Cards for the next round (initiative of slot 1, cards of slots 1 and 2). Template text is fixed in `playmat.js` (`turnPost`, `cardsPost`).

## Settings and context menu

- Playmat display settings live in the settings modal (Display tab, `displaySettingsHtml` in `campaign/playmat.js`): Hand (layout Spread, Fan or Grid, auto-sort by initiative), Card preview (Show on Hover or Ctrl+hover, Position next to the card or docked left or right, Size S to XL, Hover delay), Cards and motion (initiative badge, reduce motion). Kept per browser (`havenmap.campaign.pmset`), not in the record. Older stored settings load: zoom mode and zones are dropped, an old docked side Right becomes Position Right.
- Card preview (2026-11-17, `campaign/preview.js`, one module for every tab): a floating picture beside the card under the pointer, or docked at a window side. It ignores the pointer (no flicker, never clipped by a scrolling box) and lives in the body, outside the playmat redraw (`morph.js` reuses unkeyed elements of the `.pm`, so a layer inside it was recycled as the next modal). Triggers: hover after the delay (default Short, 150 ms); moving on to the next card shows it at once; Ctrl shows at once; with "Ctrl+hover" only Ctrl shows it. Z pins the card under the pointer; Z again, Esc or a click elsewhere closes it; the context menu Zoom pins too. Touch: a long press pins, except on cards whose long press opens the context menu (use its Zoom); on the card tabs a tap pins. Size: the long edge is a share of the window height (`SHARE`: S .40, M .52, L .64, XL .78), the same for every card kind; character mats 1.3 times. The fan's in-place zoom uses the same size (`zoomScale`). Content: pictures (`data-img`), ability cards with their tokens and stickers laid over the picture, items as a scaled copy with their badges, attack modifier cards as a copy of the built card (`pmPreview` in `app.js`). Clicks never zoom: a click on a card always does its action.
- Unique attack-modifier cards (baked images) get the class badge drawn over their lower-left corner when a perk adds them (`buildUnique` in `scripts/amd/card.js`).
- Roll mode (2026-11-23, Display tab, card Drawing): GeekRoller (default) or This app, per browser, for every draw of the campaign page. See `decisions.md` 2026-11-23 and `campaign/rollmode.js`. New draw or roll buttons must carry `onlyApp()` or `onlyGeek()`, and the phone layout needs the same.
- The settings modal tabs are in the order Display, Campaign file, Profile, Shortcuts. The Display tab was called "Mercenary tab" until 2026-11-17.
- The settings modal is 880 px wide (full width on narrow screens). Its Shortcuts tab lists two columns (groups flow into two CSS columns, one column below 560 px).
- Roll buttons: the Modifiers view's roll code is the gold roll code tile (`.amcode`). The Items tab uses the merc journal style (`.btn6.rollbtn`, "BGG code for roll"), and the scenario roller is an icon button. Copy feedback: "Copied" on the tile or the button label.
- Portrait crops are looked up by class slug (`matSlugOf` in `scripts/campaign/cardImages.js`), not by the mat's file name. The focus table is `scripts/games/portrait-focus.json`, keyed by slug.
- Markers: sticker and token pictures come from `stickerImage` and `tokenImage` (`scripts/campaign/classTokens.js`). Token snapping (2026-11-27, `scripts/campaign/tokenSnap.js`, circle positions from `scripts/games/card_token_slots.js`): a character token placed by click, dropped from the picker or moved by drag on an ability card snaps to the nearest free token circle within 1.2 circle radii (0.6 of the diameter); the dragged token previews the snapped position. Arrows (buttons, and the arrow keys with a marker selected in the editor): on a card with circles a token hops to the next free circle of its own track in that direction (a track is one tokens action of the card code, `t` in the circle data; Spellweaver Ice Armor has a top and a bottom track, Nightshroud Terror Blade two one-circle tracks), and stays when none is free; stickers, items and cards without circles move 2 percent. With no modal open, pointing at a playmat card (hand, active area, play slot, prescription) and pressing an arrow key hops the last token on the track of the hovered half (nearest to the pointer within the half) the same way. Stickers and items do not snap. The card context menu (hand, active area, prescription) has "Move token forward" (or "Move top token forward" and "Move bottom token forward" when both halves hold a token): one circle forward on the token's track, disabled at the end of the track (`forwardSlot` in `tokenSnap.js`). The record keeps only x and y in percent, no slot index.
- The settings modal has four tabs: Profile (your name), Campaign file (load, export, clear), Display, and Shortcuts (generated from the `SHORTCUTS` list in `campaign/app.js`; update it when a shortcut changes).
- Keys on the object under the mouse pointer (not focus), read at key press from the last mouse position: ability card D (discard), L (Lost), A (active area); item D (toggle spent and available), L (consumed); attack modifier D (on a placed card: discard; on a deck stack: draw one to the discard, as a click does). Ignored in edit mode for attack modifiers. The item context menu has a State section with the same three states.
- Context menu (`ctxmenu.js`): right click, Menu key, Shift+F10, long press on touch and pen. Cards: enlarge, marker, move to hand, active area, play slots (hand cards only), discard, lost, start and end of hand. Piles: recover top, move whole pile. Counters: +5, -5, reset. Every entry calls the same function as the normal UI.

## Phone view

- Used at 700 px wide or less, or 500 px high or less (a phone held sideways): `phone.js`, `phone.css`, same media query as `html.phone` (`touch.css`). Tabs Cards, Modifiers, Items, Info at the bottom (a rail at the left when sideways). Burger menu opens the same modals as the dock.
- Top bar: the name opens a sheet to switch character or add one; the pencil opens Edit character (bottom sheet).
- Cards: play area, active, hand, discard and lost. Sideways: play, active and piles in one row, the hand below. Tap a card for a bottom sheet with zones to move it to; tap a played card to flip; long press shows the card preview; Ini and Lvl sort the hand.
- Modifiers: the playmat's Modifiers view (`amViewHtml`) laid out for the phone (draw controls, four stacks a row, Top and Bottom, discard and active). Tap a stack draws it; hold and move a card places it; holding still shows the preview.
- Items: the item pictures, spent and consumed labelled; a tap opens the item modal.
- Info: traits; XP, gold and inherited perks with [-] value [+] steppers.
- Not on the phone: class change from the hand editor is the same modal as on the playmat, not checked at phone width.

## History logging

Every edit goes through `commit` and is logged (`changelog.js`). Small counter changes within 4 seconds are folded into one line ("gold 42 to 47 (+5)"). Modifier deck actions are written directly by `addLog` in `app.js`, since the deck state is not in the record.

## Known gaps

- Touch drag of modifier cards uses pointer events; not tried on touch hardware.
- Hand fan and active area size from their containers, not `--cu`.
- Long press, touch drag and the phone view were tested in Playwright with touch emulation (2026-11-17), not on a real device. The 2026-11-14 layout changes are not browser-tested (see `status.md`).
- Item and enhancement price shortcuts from the old mockup are not built.
- Card active marking has no UI.
