# Current card code, one .md and one .json per class

Copies of the most current version of each class, sorted into folders by progress:

- `confirmed/`: checked by the owner against the cards.
- `wip/`: being checked now.
- `start/`: not checked yet.

Move a class's two files (`card_code_<class>.md` and `<class>-ability-cards.code.json`) between the folders as its status changes. Sources: the owner's files in import/ and tools/, the picture-read drafts, or the Secretariat conversions in tools/secretariat-cards/. Do not regenerate this folder from the sources without moving the files back first: the copies in the folders are the ones to edit.
