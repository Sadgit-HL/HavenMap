Attack modifier deck: quick access, five options

Dashed coral outline = the new element. Everything else is the current playmat, shown schematically. The full AMD modal stays for roll codes, bless/curse editing and returning from the discard.

A. Top bar chip: deck and draw in one place

A compact widget in the top bar (about 190 px). One click on the card back draws; the last drawn card shows beside it; the numbers are deck and discard. Adv and Dis draw two. The dots button opens the full modal. Cost: roughly one stat box of top-bar space.

Matches the table metaphor. A card back in the pile column draws on click; its own small discard shows the last drawn face on top. Count under each. A shuffle and a menu button sit below the pair. Cost: the pile column gets one more cell (the three share its height).

C. Edge drawer: a slim tab that expands

A 34 px tab on the right edge of the board with the deck count and a draw button; hover or click expands the drawer shown here, with draw per card type and bless and curse steppers. Collapsed it costs almost nothing. Cost: the open drawer overlays the piles.

D. Popover from a top bar button: last draws and bless/curse

The top bar only gets a small deck button with the count. A click opens a popover under it with a strip of the last drawn cards (newest large), Draw, Adv, Dis, Shuffle and the bless/curse steppers. Closes on outside click. Cost: about 60 px in the top bar, nothing when closed.
