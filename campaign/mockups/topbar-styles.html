Top bar styles

Five layouts for the character header. Same content in each: portrait, name and class, traits, level, XP, gold, inherited perks, class tokens, the three BGG code buttons (AMD, Turn, Cards) and settings. Counters use the spin box. Hover the icon-only buttons in E and the Copy menu in D.

A. Command bar

One slim row. Level badge sits on the portrait, XP is a labelled bar, gold and inherited perks are spin boxes with small captions. The three BGG buttons are one segmented control with a "BGG" caption, so it is obvious they all copy a code. Traits move into the subtitle line to save height.

B. Two tier

Identity on top (bigger portrait, name, traits), a quiet toolbar underneath for the numbers on the left and the actions on the right. The toolbar row reads like an app toolbar, with the BGG buttons grouped and labelled. Tokens move to the toolbar so the identity row stays calm.

D. Ring and Copy menu

Level and XP share one ring around the portrait, so the badge and the bar disappear. Gold and inherited perks are pills with the coin icon. The three BGG buttons collapse into one "Copy BGG code" menu with a description under each entry. Cleanest and shortest, but one extra click per copy.

E. Icon toolbar

Dense and quiet. The BGG buttons become icon-only squares with tooltips, XP progress is a thin line along the bottom edge of the bar with the numbers beside the level, and the portrait is a small rounded square. Fastest to scan, least labelled, so it needs the tooltips to teach the icons.
