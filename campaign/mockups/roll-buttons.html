Roll and BGG code buttons

Ten variants of the three buttons in the merc tab: Turn and Cards (BGG code, top bar) and Roll code (modifier deck). Each variant is one row, the three buttons side by side at the size they have in the tab. The first row is the current look for comparison. Hover any button to see its hover state; they are all live.
